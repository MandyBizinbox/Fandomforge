Creator Studio smoke checklist

1. Catalogue → Product Preview → Start Designing opens the full-screen studio.
2. Creator Console sidebar/topbar is fully covered while Studio is open.
3. Product title/description/brand/category edit from the left Details drawer.
4. Variants are selected from the right panel; no template picker is exposed.
5. Design tools render in the centre once required variants are selected.
6. Mockups open from the left rail and generated images can be chosen for storefront gallery.
7. Pricing is editable from the right panel and existing production/commission calculations remain visible.
8. Edit/Preview top toggle works.
9. Save Product creates/updates through existing `/products` endpoints.
10. Admin/SuperAdmin Product Builder remains on ProductBuilderV4.
